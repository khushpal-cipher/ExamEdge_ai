Coaching institute portal

For the owner, admins and teachers
  • Batches & subjects: create batches, subjects, chapters; invite teachers and students
  • Notes: upload notes and PDFs; they appear in the right subject for the right batch
  • Tests: mix MCQ, numerical and theory questions; set marks, negative marking and timing
  • Checking: MCQs marked automatically; teachers check theory answers and give marks
  • Two ranks: rank for each test and overall rank (average % of all tests)
  • Student tracking: click any name to see marks, ranks, weak chapters and practice
  • AI drafts: quizzes, summaries and flashcards made by AI, approved by teachers

Student portal

Login with Google, after the institute invites them
  • Subjects: every subject with the institute's notes inside
  • AI tutor: ask doubts any time in each subject, no uploading needed
  • Answers with source: shows the exact notes page it used
  • Self practice: practice quizzes, flashcards, summaries and mind maps
  • Tests: attempt institute tests and see marks with teacher comments
  • My progress: test rank, overall rank, weak chapters, marks trend
  • Coming later: audio lessons in Hindi and other languages, animated videos
