Torque kya hota hai? Simple example se samjhao

Torque is the turning effect of a force. It tells you how strongly a force can rotate an object around a point (the axis).

τ = r × F   |   τ = r F sin θ
From your notes · Rotation – Class Notes, p. 4

Example: opening a door. Push near the hinge and it barely moves; push at the handle (larger r) and it opens easily with the same force. Same force, bigger distance, bigger torque.

ExamEdge library · Torque

JEE tip: torque is a vector. Its direction is given by the right-hand rule, and its unit is N·m.
