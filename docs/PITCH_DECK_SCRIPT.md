# Investor pitch deck script (founder, 2026-10-31, not yet built as slides)

11 slides, final wording agreed in session 63. All money figures are estimates [E] at ₹90 per $ including 18% GST and
forex on the foreign services we buy. Market figures are assumptions [A], not sourced counts.
Check before presenting: TAM/SAM inputs (3% software spend, 10,000 local coachings) are unverified; competitor columns come
from homepages only and Classplus did not load; 1-in-5 trial conversion is a guess until real trials run.

1. **Title.** ExamEdge AI, "Helping local coachings compete with big chains". Founder name.
2. **Problem.** Goal: help local coachings compete with big coachings like Aakash and Allen. No good software to track students
   and teachers; teachers make notes and tests by hand; doubts go unanswered or are solved slowly.
3. **Solution.** One portal to track students, teachers, tests and progress; AI notes and tests from the teacher's own material,
   teacher-approved; 24x7 doubt solving from the coaching's own notes with page cited; teacher-marked written answers, fair ranks;
   AI cost capped.
4. **Market.** India coaching about $7.2B (2025), about 10% growth (IMARC). TAM about ₹1,900 crore a year software spend;
   SAM about ₹360 crore (about 10,000 local/mid-size coachings x ₹3.6 lakh); SOM ₹0.7-1.8 crore in 1-2 years (20-50 coachings).
5. **Competitors.** Aakash/Allen are the customer's rivals. Table ExamEdge vs Teachmint, Edmingle, Classplus: AI from the
   coaching's own notes with page cited, teacher-approved AI tests, teacher-marked written answers + ranks, live classes (planned),
   own app (web only). Banner: "Others manage your coaching. ExamEdge puts your teachers' knowledge in front of every student."
6. **Features (live demo, about 10 min).** Upload notes, student asks a doubt, AI test approved by the teacher, student takes it,
   teacher marks and releases ranks, progress page + AI bill. Each feature framed as a gap with big chains it closes. Skip Beta
   audio/video overviews.
7. **Cost to run.** Platform about ₹11,000-16,000 a month fixed; AI at most about ₹66 per account + about ₹3,400 per coaching;
   payment fees about 3%; one free trial at most about ₹550. No tech vendor names on the slide.
8. **Unit economics (50 accounts, monthly, before GST).** Revenue ₹10,000; AI ₹6,742 worst / about ₹2,700 realistic; fee ₹300;
   profit ₹2,958 worst / about ₹7,000 realistic. 100 accounts: worst about ₹9,300. About 200 paying accounts cover fixed costs.
9. **Failed trial.** 7 days, 5 students, AI capped at $5, about ₹550 at most. One 50-account conversion (at least ₹2,958 a month)
   pays for about 5 failed trials.
10. **7 coachings** (50, 80, 100, 150, 200, 300, 500 = 1,380 accounts, monthly, bigger database): revenue ₹2,60,000 a month;
    profit after all costs about ₹1,21,000 worst (47%) / about ₹1,90,000 realistic (AI at 40% of cap); before income tax and founder pay.
11. **Ask: ₹10,000** for the AI cost of free 1-week trials: about 18 trials at worst case, about 30 if usage is lower; if 1 in 5
    convert, about 4 paying coachings. Platform fixed costs are not in the ask (founder covers them). Say the ask last.

AI cost is pay-as-you-go: the founder tops up the Anthropic balance ("tank"); each coaching draws only what its users use, up to its
cap; unused cap costs nothing and does not carry over. Keep the balance near expected real use; if it hits zero, AI stops for everyone.
